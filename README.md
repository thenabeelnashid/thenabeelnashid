## Hey, I'm Nabeel

I'm a self-taught **designer and developer**. I build websites, web apps and fully automated systems that replace manual work — from the first pixel to the infrastructure that runs it.

Most of what I ship lives at **[Veloredge](https://veloredge.com)**, where I design and engineer products end to end. I care about clear hierarchy, fast interfaces and systems that quietly do their job.

## What I do

**Design** — Interfaces with a clear focal point and a single primary action. Typography, spacing and color treated as a system, not decoration.

**Develop** — Production-ready web apps on **Next.js** and **Supabase** — typed, accessible, fast, and built to scale from day one.

**Automate** — Python pipelines and scheduled jobs that replace manual work and keep content, data and deployments moving without intervention.
